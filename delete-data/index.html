Delete your data

Last updated: 6 October 2026

SantaDial is provided by Pete Evia-Rhodes, United Kingdom. You can delete your family's SantaDial data at any time, in the app or by email.

Option 1: in the app (instant)

  1. Open SantaDial and tap Grown-ups on Santa's cottage.
  2. Answer the times-tables question.
  3. Tap your child's name.
  4. Tap Erase and confirm.
  5. Repeat for each child. This deletes everything you told Santa about them straight away. To also delete your anonymous account and call count, use option 2.

Option 2: by email

Email whatireallyreallywant2@gmail.com with the subject "SantaDial delete my data". Tell us your children's first names as entered in the app so we can find your records. We'll delete them within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Everything you told Santa about each child: first name, how to say it, age, siblings, pets, wishes, proud moments and traditions.
  • Your family's anonymous account.
  • Your call count records.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Call records held by our voice partner ElevenLabs are kept only for its retention period (we set this to the shortest available) and then deleted automatically.
  • Purchase records (when purchases are available) are kept by Google Play or the App Store under their own policies, as the law requires.
  • "Record yours" name recordings never leave your phone and are deleted when you leave that screen.
