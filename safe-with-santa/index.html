FOR GROWN-UPS

Safe with Santa

Every parent asks the same things first. Here are the straight answers, in one minute. The full detail is in the grown-ups' guide and our privacy policy.

What Santa will never do

  • Promise presents. He listens to wishes and keeps the magic going.
  • Ask for personal details. No surname, address, school, photos or contact details. Santa is told never to ask.
  • Use the naughty list as a threat. No threats and no "I am watching you".
  • Ask your child to keep a secret from you. Never.
  • Chat about anything and everything. He stays in character as Santa.
  • Pretend to know things you did not tell him. He only knows what a grown-up typed in.

What you control

  • Setup, your children's details and purchases sit behind a grown-ups check. Children do not create accounts.
  • Before anything is sent, the app shows you what is shared and with whom. You can say no.
  • You choose what Santa knows. See it, change it or erase it at any time.
  • You can mute or end the call whenever you like. The screen always shows whether the microphone is on.
  • Calls come in packs, paid once. No subscription, nothing renews.

Is it really Santa?

We are straight with grown-ups: Santa's voice is AI. That is how he can listen and answer live, for every family at once. AI can occasionally mishear or say something a little odd, especially in a noisy room, so stay nearby.

Your child's voice

The microphone is only used during a call. Your child's voice goes to our voice partner, ElevenLabs, so Santa can hear and reply. We do not record calls or keep transcripts. ElevenLabs may keep call records for a limited time under its own settings, which we are setting to the shortest period available. There are no adverts, no selling of data and no tracking tools in the app. You can erase everything in the Grown-ups area, or email us.

ELF TIP

Stay in the room for the call. It is more fun with you there.

If something worries your child

If a child shares something sensitive, Santa responds kindly, does not press for details and encourages them to talk to a grown-up they trust.
