SANTADIAL GUIDE

Call Father Christmas: how it works

Many families in the UK want to ring Father Christmas himself, not play a recording. SantaDial is a live voice call with Santa. Your child speaks, he listens and replies to what they actually said, using their name. Santa's voice is AI, and a grown-up sets everything up first.

  • Live, not a recording
  • Knows their name
  • Up to about 5 minutes

Three ways it can happen

He knows their name

A first name is enough. Add how to say it, a pet or something they are proud of, and Santa brings it up himself.

He answers back, live

Your child asks about the reindeer or tells him about their day. Santa replies to that, so no two calls are the same.

Santa rings you

In the UK you can book a time, and Father Christmas calls your mobile.

How to set it up

  1. Get the appSantaDial is live on Google Play now and coming very soon to iPhone.
  2. Tell Santa a littleA grown-up adds a first name and, if they like, a few details. You see what is shared before anything is sent.
  3. Call, or book a timeCall straight away, or pick a time and have Santa ring your mobile.
  4. Stay nearbySit close and let your child do the talking.
ELF TIP

Tell Santa one thing your child is proud of. He loves to ask about it.

Good to know

There is no camera and no video of your child. Setup, your children's details and purchases sit behind a grown-ups check. Santa does not ask children for personal information and does not promise presents. Read more on the Safe with Santa page.

Questions

Can I phone Father Christmas?

With SantaDial a grown-up sets up a live call in the app, and your child talks to Santa, who answers what they actually say. In the UK you can also book a time and Santa rings your mobile.

Is it a real person?

No. Santa's voice is AI. That is how he can answer live, by name, at any time of day. A grown-up sets everything up first.

Is it a recording?

No. It is a live conversation, so Santa replies to what your child says rather than playing a message made in advance.

How long is a call?

A call lasts up to about 5 minutes, and Santa wraps up kindly at the end.
