SantaDial is a live voice call with Santa. Your child talks, Santa listens and answers what they actually said, by name. You set it up, you stay in charge, and you get the best seat in the house.

From our family to yours

We are parents too. We built SantaDial the way we would want it for our own children: full of wonder for them, and no surprises for us.

So here is everything, plainly. How it works, what you control, and what happens to your child's voice.

The SantaDial family
What a live call is

A real chat with Santa, not a recording.

Most Santa apps play a recording, or send a video made in advance. This is a conversation. Your child can ask about the reindeer, talk about their day or change the subject completely, and Santa goes with them.

He answers back

Santa replies to what your child says, so no two calls are the same.

He knows their name

And the things you told him, like a pet or something they are proud of.

Voice only

There is no camera and no video of your child. Calls last up to about 5 minutes.

Santa can ring you

In the UK you can book a time and Santa calls your mobile.

What you control

You hold the keys to the sleigh.

  • You set it up. Children do not create accounts. Setup, your children's details and purchases sit behind a grown-ups check.
  • You say yes first. Before anything is sent, the app shows you what is shared and with whom, and you can say no.
  • You choose what Santa knows. See it, change it or erase it at any time.
  • You can mute or end the call whenever you like. The call screen always shows whether the microphone is on.
  • You decide what you spend. Calls come in packs, paid for once. No subscription, nothing renews.
Elf tip

Stay in the room for the call. It is more fun with you there, and they will want to tell you all about it.

What Santa knows

Only what you type in.

A first name is enough. If you like, add how to say it, their age, brothers and sisters, pets, wishes, things they are proud of and family traditions.

We never ask for a surname, address, school, photos or contact details, and Santa is told never to ask your child for them either.

What Santa will not do

Kind, every time.

  • He will not promise presents. He listens to wishes and keeps the magic going.
  • He will not use the naughty list as a threat, or say he is watching.
  • He will not ask your child to keep a secret from you.
  • He will not chat about anything and everything. He stays Santa.
  • He will not pretend to know things you did not tell him.

If a child shares something worrying or sad, Santa responds kindly, does not press for details and encourages them to talk to a grown-up they trust.

Is it really Santa?

We are straight with grown-ups.

Santa's voice is AI. That is how he can listen and answer live, at any time of day, for every family at once. On the call he is simply Santa, so the magic is yours to keep or explain as you see fit.

AI can occasionally mishear or say something a little odd, especially in a noisy room. One more reason to sit nearby.

Your child's voice

Heard by Santa. Not kept by us.

  • The microphone is only used during a call. Your child's voice goes to our voice partner, ElevenLabs, so Santa can hear and reply.
  • We do not record calls or keep transcripts. ElevenLabs may keep call records for a limited time under its own settings, which we are setting to the shortest period available.
  • No adverts, no selling data, no tracking tools in the app.
  • You can erase everything in the Grown-ups area, or email us and we will delete the lot.

The full detail is in our privacy policy, written in plain English.

Language and names

He says it properly.

Santa speaks English, with the words of your country, so "Father Christmas" and "mum" sound right in the UK. Names matter most: type how to say your child's name and hear Santa say it before the call.

Five little tips

Making the call magical.

  1. One proud thingA swimming badge, a kind deed, a wobbly tooth. Santa loves to ask about it.
  2. A quiet roomTelly off, so Santa can hear every word.
  3. Let them talkSit close, but try not to answer for them. Shy ones warm up after the first question.
  4. A question ready"What do the reindeer eat?" breaks the ice nicely.
  5. Watch their faceNot the phone. That look is the best bit.
Questions grown-ups ask

Good to know

What age is it for?

Mainly children aged 2 to 12. It is for anyone who loves Christmas, and a carer or family member can also set it up for an older person, for example someone with a learning disability.

Can my child use it on their own?

It is designed for a grown-up to set up and be there. Setup and purchases are behind a grown-ups check.

What if my child says something personal?

Santa does not ask for personal information. If a child shares something sensitive, he responds kindly and points them to a trusted grown-up. We do not keep a recording or transcript of the call.

What does it cost?

Your first call is a free sample call, once per family. After that it is 3 calls for £3.99 in the UK, paid once with no subscription. The price in your own store may differ.

How do I delete our details?

In the app, go to Grown-ups, tap the child and choose Erase. To delete everything, email us from the support page.
