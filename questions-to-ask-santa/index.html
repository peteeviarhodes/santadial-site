SANTADIAL GUIDE

Questions to ask Santa

A good question makes a live call with Santa even better. Here are fifteen that children love, for ages 2 to 12. Pick one or two before the call so your child has something ready. Santa answers in his own words and often asks something back.

Fifteen questions to try

  1. What do the reindeer eat?
  2. How do the reindeer know the way?
  3. What is the weather like at the North Pole?
  4. What do the elves do all day?
  5. How do you get round the whole world in one night?
  6. What is your favourite biscuit?
  7. What do you eat on Christmas Eve?
  8. What is Mrs Claus doing right now?
  9. How old are you?
  10. Where do you keep your sleigh?
  11. What is the best bit of being Santa?
  12. What is the funniest thing an elf has done?
  13. Do you have a favourite carol?
  14. What can I do to help at home before Christmas?
  15. What is your favourite colour?
ELF TIP

Write your child's favourite question on a card. Shy ones can read it out.

For younger children (2 to 5)

Keep it simple. Ask about one animal, one colour or one favourite food. Let them answer in a word or two.

For older children (6 to 12)

Older children enjoy the clever ones: how the sleigh works, how the reindeer fly or what the elves are making. Santa stays in character and answers with a twinkle.

Questions Santa will not answer

Santa does not promise specific presents. He also does not ask your child for an address, a school or other personal details. If a child shares something worrying, he responds kindly and encourages them to talk to a grown-up they trust. See Safe with Santa.

Questions grown-ups ask

What should my child ask Santa?

Most children like to ask about the reindeer, the elves, the weather at the North Pole and how Santa gets round the world in one night. A question ready on the day helps shy children start.

What if my child goes quiet?

That is fine. Shy children often warm up after the first question. Santa waits patiently and may ask something gentle to help.

Will Santa promise a present?

No. He listens to wishes and keeps the magic going, but he does not promise presents.
