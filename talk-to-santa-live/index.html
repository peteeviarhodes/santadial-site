SANTADIAL GUIDE

Talk to Santa live: what kids can ask him

A live call with Santa is a real back and forth. Your child speaks, Santa listens and replies to what they actually said. It is not a recording or a voicemail. Santa's voice is AI, and a grown-up sets everything up first.

What children like to ask

Most children arrive with a question ready. Common ones are about the reindeer, the elves, the weather at the North Pole, what Santa eats on Christmas Eve and how he gets round the whole world in one night. Santa answers each one in his own words and often asks something back.

What Santa already knows

Before the call, a grown-up tells Santa a first name and, if they like, how to say it, an age, brothers and sisters, pets, wishes and things the child is proud of. Santa brings those up himself, so the call feels personal from the first hello.

ELF TIP

Tell Santa one thing your child is proud of. He loves to ask about it.

What Santa will not do

  • He does not promise presents. He listens to wishes and keeps the magic going.
  • He does not ask children for personal information such as an address or a school.
  • He does not chat about anything and everything. He stays in character as Santa.
  • If a child shares something sensitive, he responds kindly and points them to a trusted grown-up.

Helping the call go well

Choose a quiet room and let your child do the talking. Sit nearby, but try not to answer for them. A call lasts up to about 5 minutes, and Santa wraps up kindly at the end. You can call straight away, or book a time and have Santa ring your mobile.

ELF TIP

Check how to say your child's name before the call. You can even hear Santa say it first.
