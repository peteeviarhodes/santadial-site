Questions parents ask

Is it a recording?

No. Santa answers live. He listens to what your child says and replies to it, so every call goes a little differently.

Is Santa a real person?

No. Santa's voice is AI, and we say so up front. A grown-up sets everything up and stays in charge.

How long is a call?

A call lasts up to about 5 minutes. Santa wraps up kindly, and may run slightly over so he can answer your child's last question.

How much does it cost?

Your first call is a free sample call, once per family. It is a short taste of the full call, which lasts up to about 5 minutes. After that it is 3 calls for £3.99 in the UK, paid once with no subscription. The price in your own store may differ.

What does Santa need to know?

Just a first name. You can also tell him how to say it, their age, brothers and sisters, pets, wishes and what they are proud of. We never ask for a surname, address, school or photos.

Can Santa ring us instead?

Yes. In the grown-ups area you confirm your own UK mobile number with a text code and choose a time. Santa rings you then, and it uses one call.

Is it safe for children?

Grown-ups set it up behind a grown-ups check. Santa never asks children for personal information. If a child shares something sensitive he responds kindly and points them to a trusted grown-up. The app has no adverts and no tracking.

What ages is it for?

Mainly children aged 2 to 12. A carer or family member can also set it up for an older person, for example someone with a learning disability.
