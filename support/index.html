Common questions

How do I get to the grown-ups area? On Santa's cottage, tap Grown-ups and answer the times-tables question.

How do I change what Santa knows? Grown-ups, tap your child, then tap any answer to change it.

How do I buy more calls? Grown-ups, then Get more. Payments are one-off through the App Store or Google Play; there's no subscription.

How does "Santa rings you" work? In Grown-ups, confirm your own mobile number with a text code and choose a time. Santa rings that number at that time. It uses one call.

A call didn't connect or a payment didn't go through. Email us with the date and time and we'll put it right.

How do I delete our data? Grown-ups, then Delete all our data. See also deleting your data.
