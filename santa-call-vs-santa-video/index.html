Santa call or Santa video: which is better?

Both can be lovely. The right choice depends on your child and what you want from it. Here is an honest comparison.

A video message

A personalised Santa video is recorded in advance and slots in your child's name and a few details. It is something you can keep, share with grandparents and watch again every year. The limit is that your child cannot talk back. It is a message, so it works best for children who like to watch.

A live call

On a live call your child speaks and Santa answers. He picks up on what they say, so a funny answer or a surprise question gets a real reply. Children who love to chat usually get the most from it. The trade off is that there is nothing to keep afterwards. A call is a moment, not a file, and we do not keep recordings of it.

Which should you choose?

If you want a keepsake to share, choose a video. If you want your child to be part of the conversation, choose a call. Some families do both, a video to keep and a call for the live moment.
